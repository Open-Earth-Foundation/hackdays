CAPAG Funder Scan

Brazil's Treasury rates every municipality's creditworthiness. CityCatalyst knows its emissions and climate risk. This joins the two — the financing market funders can't currently see, and a deployable, leverage-sorted pipeline. Real data, 89-city sample. This is a standalone prototype — the live tool covers all municipalities with real headroom.

Real data: CAPAG ratings + sub-indicators (Tesouro Nacional, Nov-2025, ODbL) · SEEG/SINIR/SNIS emissions + CCRA climate risk (CityCatalyst Global API). Population and ticket/headroom magnitudes are modeled estimates; the debt ratio, headroom %, and tier are real. Indicative screening signal — the definitive CAPAG is computed by STN when a municipality applies for guaranteed credit. Not a credit decision. Prototype built from the Open Earth hackday capag-funder-scan; does not modify that repo.
