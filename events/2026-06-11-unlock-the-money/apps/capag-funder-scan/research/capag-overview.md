# CAPAG methodology — the 5-minute version

**What it is:** STN's payment-capacity rating diagnosing fiscal health of states/municipalities seeking Union-guaranteed credit. Legal basis: Portaria Normativa MF 1.583/2023 (in force from 2024, amended by 1.764/2024, operationalized by Portaria STN 217/2024).

## The three indicators

| # | Indicator | Formula | Source | Partial grades |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Endividamento (DC)** | Dívida consolidada bruta / Receita Corrente Líquida | RGF anexo 02 | A/B/C |
| 2 | **Poupança corrente (PC)** | Despesa corrente / Receita corrente, 3-year weighted avg (50/30/20) | DCA anexos I-C, I-D (3 years) | A/B/C |
| 3 | **Liquidez relativa (LR)** | (Disponibilidade de caixa bruta − Obrigações financeiras) / RCL | RGF anexo 05 | A/B/C (was A/C pre-2024) |

## Final rating

Combination of the three partials → A, B, C, or D. Then the **ICF** (Siconfi accounting-quality ranking) modifies it:

- A or B entity with **Aicf** (≥95th percentile accounting quality) → **A+** or **B+**
- **Dicf / Eicf** entities → ineligible for Union guarantees regardless of indicators

So the full scale: **A+ · A · B+ · B · C · D**, plus **n.d.** (not calculated — bad/missing Siconfi data) and **n.e.** in the files.

## What the rating gates

CAPAG A/B (+ other LRF limits) → eligible for credit operations with Union guarantee. CAPAG C/D → blocked from the federal guarantee channel; needs grants, blended finance, or non-guaranteed credit.

## Validity & cadence

Under the 2023 rules a rating has no fixed annual validity — it holds "até que sejam atualizadas as fontes de informação" (until the underlying Siconfi filings refresh, in practice ~annually). The open-data files are published "quadrimestral" (declared, not guaranteed; ~3 drops/year observed).

## The 2023/2024 break (don't build time series across it)

The 2023 reform revoked the 2022 portarias (ME 5.623, 6.039, 9.266), reformulated liquidity, added B partial for LR, introduced ICF/A+/B+/D, and started excluding bad-data municipalities (n.d.). **Ratings from 2018–2023 files and 2024+ files are not comparable.**

## Non-binding disclaimer (put this in any product)

Published figures — STN's own files included — are indicative. The definitive CAPAG is computed by STN at the moment a municipality applies for a guaranteed credit operation. Any derived tool is a screening/prioritization signal, not a credit decision.
