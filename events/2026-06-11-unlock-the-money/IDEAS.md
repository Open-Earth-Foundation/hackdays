## 1. Political Will Score

**Proposed by:** Carole

One of the blockers we hear from funders is TRUST. "Will this city still prioritize climate after the next election?" Nobody has a good answer, so finance doesn't flow.

Build an indicator that monitors publicly available signals — budget trends, political cycles, staff turnover, local news — and gives each city a simple, continuously updated score: how likely is this commitment to hold?

Think credit rating, but for political will.

**For:** Development banks (BNDES), climate finance alliances (CCFLA Brazil)
**Revenue angle:** If BNDES uses this to allocate capital, OEF becomes essential infrastructure in the deal flow.
**Starter data:** Brazilian city budgets (portal da transparencia), election calendars, climate plan publications from CityCatalyst.

## 2. Plan Survival Tracker

What happens to a climate action plan after it's published? Most funders have zero visibility. Build a lightweight monitor that tracks:
- Budget allocations vs. plan commitments
- Staff/department changes in climate offices
- News sentiment about climate priorities
- Milestone completion (actual vs. planned)

**For:** Institutional investors, development banks post-investment
**Revenue angle:** Subscription data service. Recurring revenue from funders who already deployed capital and need ongoing assurance.

---

## 3. Funder Match Engine

Cities have needs (infra projects, technical assistance, capacity building). Funders have instruments (grants, concessional loans, guarantees, TA facilities). The matching is 100% manual today.

Build a recommendation engine: given a city's profile + climate plan from CityCatalyst, suggest the best-fit funding instruments and generate a first-draft application.

**For:** Cities looking for funding, OEF partnerships team
**Revenue angle:** Premium CityCatalyst feature. Direct partnership value with CCFLA/IDB.

---

## 4. AI Grant Writer

OEF and partner cities spend weeks writing grant applications. Most of the content already exists in CityCatalyst (emissions data, action plans, implementation timelines, co-benefits).

Build a tool that takes a city's CityCatalyst data + a grant template and produces a first-draft application.

**For:** OEF internal (saves weeks of work), cities, consultancies
**Revenue angle:** Direct time savings for OEF + potential paid service for cities.

---

## 5. MRV-as-a-Service Dashboard

Measurement, Reporting, and Verification is what funders need *after* they deploy capital. "Is this city actually delivering on its commitments?"

Build a lightweight MRV dashboard that pulls from CityCatalyst data and shows progress against committed targets.

**For:** Funders post-investment, city governments for reporting
**Revenue angle:** Subscription service. Recurring revenue from funders who need continuous verification.

---

## 6. City Credit Brief Generator

For any city in CityCatalyst, auto-generate a 2-page "investment brief" that a funder can hand to their credit committee. Includes: emissions profile, action plan summary, institutional capacity indicators, risk factors, co-benefit metrics.

**For:** CCFLA partners, development banks, impact investors
**Revenue angle:** Premium feature. Direct ask from CCFLA partners already.
