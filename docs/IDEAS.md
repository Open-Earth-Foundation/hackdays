## 1. Political Will Score

**Proposed by:** Carole (Head of Partnerships)

One of the blockers we hear from funders is TRUST. "Will this city still prioritize climate after the next election?" Nobody has a good answer, so finance doesn't flow.

Build an indicator that monitors publicly available signals — budget trends, political cycles, staff turnover, local news — and gives each city a simple, continuously updated score: how likely is this commitment to hold?

Think credit rating, but for political will.

**First use case:** Brazil with CCFLA Brazil, with BNDES as the target partner.

**Revenue angle:** Funder tool. If BNDES uses this to allocate capital, OEF becomes essential infrastructure in the deal flow.

## 2. Plan Survival Tracker

What happens to a climate action plan after it's published? Most funders have no visibility. Build a lightweight monitor that tracks:
- Budget allocations vs. plan commitments
- Staff/department changes in climate offices
- News sentiment about climate priorities
- Milestone completion (actual vs. planned)

**Revenue angle:** Subscription data service for development banks and institutional investors.

---

## 3. Funder Match Engine

Cities have needs (infra projects, technical assistance, capacity building). Funders have instruments (grants, concessional loans, guarantees, TA facilities). The matching is manual today.

Build a recommendation engine: given a city's profile + climate plan from CityCatalyst, suggest the best-fit funding instruments and generate a first-draft application.

**Revenue angle:** Premium CityCatalyst feature. Partnership with CCFLA/IDB.

---

## 4. AI Grant Writer

OEF and partner cities spend weeks writing grant applications. Most of the content is already in CityCatalyst (emissions data, action plans, implementation timelines).

Build a tool that takes a city's CityCatalyst data + a grant template and produces a first-draft application.

**Revenue angle:** Direct time savings for OEF + potential paid service for cities.

## 5. MRV-as-a-Service Dashboard

Measurement, Reporting, and Verification is what funders need *after* they deploy capital. Build a lightweight MRV dashboard that pulls from CityCatalyst data and shows: is this city delivering on its commitments?

**Revenue angle:** Subscription service for funders who have already invested. Recurring revenue.

---

## 6. City Credit Brief Generator

For any city in CityCatalyst, auto-generate a 2-page "investment brief" that a funder can use in their credit committee. Includes: emissions profile, action plan summary, institutional capacity indicators, risk factors, co-benefit metrics.

**Revenue angle:** Premium feature. Direct ask from CCFLA partners.
