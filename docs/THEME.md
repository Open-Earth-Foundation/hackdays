# Hackday Q2 2026 — "Unlock the Money"

## The Problem

Climate finance exists. $100B+ per year flows to climate projects globally. But it doesn't reach cities — especially in the Global South. The #1 blocker funders tell us: **trust**.

- "Will this city still prioritize climate after the next election?"
- "Will the plan survive a budget cut?"
- "Is there institutional capacity to execute?"

Nobody has a good, data-driven answer. So finance doesn't flow.

## The Challenge

Build something in 24 hours that helps **unlock climate finance** by solving a trust, transparency, or decision-support problem for funders, cities, or OEF's revenue pipeline.

## Revenue Framing

Every project should connect to at least one of these:

| Angle | Example |
|-------|---------|
| **Funder tools** | Help BNDES, CCFLA, IDB trust cities enough to deploy capital. OEF becomes the trust infrastructure. |
| **Product features** | Premium CityCatalyst capabilities that cities or consultancies pay for. |
| **Data services** | Scoring, analytics, MRV-as-a-service that partners subscribe to. |
| **Partnership enablers** | Tools that make Carole's partnership conversations convert faster. |

## Constraints

- **24 hours.** Ship something demoable. Ugly is fine. Broken is fine. Invisible is not.
- **Teams of 2-4.** Collaborate. Pair-program with Claude. Divide and conquer.
- **Cursor + Claude only.** No Copilot, no Windsurf, no ChatGPT coding. We test one stack under pressure.
- **One repo.** Your app lives in `apps/your-team-name/`. Push to branch `hackday/your-team-name`.
- **Demo = 5 min.** Show what it does, who it's for, and how it connects to revenue.

## Target Partners (for context)

These are real relationships OEF is nurturing. If your tool helps close one of these, it's immediately valuable:

- **BNDES** (Brazilian Development Bank) — financing climate projects in Brazilian cities
- **CCFLA Brazil** (Cities Climate Finance Leadership Alliance) — aggregator of city-level climate commitments
- **Net Zero Cities (EU)** — 112 European cities with climate plans that need implementation support
- **IDB** (Inter-American Development Bank) — Latin America climate infra
